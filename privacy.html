Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Recit – Screen Recorder & Video Editor uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: your recordings stay on your phone, and the app has no account and no servers of its own.

The short version

  • Your screen recordings, edits and projects are stored only on your device. Recit never uploads them. They leave your phone only when you share them.
  • There's no account and no sign-up. We don't ask for your name, email, contacts or location.
  • The camera, microphone and screen are used only when you turn those features on, and Android asks your permission first.
  • Recit is free and shows ads from Start.io, which collects device information such as your advertising ID. App settings come from Firebase Remote Config (Google).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, manage permissions, delete recordings and uninstall at any time.

Who we are

Recit – Screen Recorder & Video Editor ("Recit", "the app") is developed and published by JJ Studios ("we", "us", "our"), an independent developer based in India. We are responsible for this app and this policy. You can reach us as described in Contact us.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Recit Android app (package recit.videoeditor.screenrecorder) distributed on Google Play, and to this website. It does not cover other apps or websites, including those of advertisers whose ads you see in Recit or the third-party services described below, which have their own privacy policies.

What we don't collect

We don't run servers that receive data from the app. We (JJ Studios) do not collect, receive or store:

  • your screen recordings, edited videos, voice-overs, music or images;
  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details (Recit has no accounts);
  • your contacts, messages, call logs or calendar;
  • your precise location (Recit does not ask for location permission);
  • what you record, or what you see on screen while recording.

The only personal information we receive directly is what you choose to send us, for example when you email us for support.

Permissions and why we need them

Android asks for your permission before Recit can use the camera, microphone, notifications or the screen. Optional permissions can be refused; the related feature simply won't be available. You can change them at any time in your phone's Settings > Apps > Recit > Permissions.

PermissionWhat it's used for
Screen capture (Android's screen recording consent)To record your screen. Android asks you each time you start a recording and shows that your screen is being recorded. Recording stops when you stop it.
Microphone (optional)To record your voice during a screen recording, and to record voice-overs in the editor. Only while you are recording.
Camera (optional)To show the facecam bubble during a recording. The camera picture is only drawn into your recording on your device. It is not saved separately or sent anywhere.
Display over other appsTo show the floating recording controls and the facecam bubble on top of the app you are recording.
NotificationsTo show the recording and export notifications, with their Stop and Cancel buttons.
Foreground services, wake lockTo keep a recording or an export running reliably while you use other apps or the screen turns off.
InternetTo load ads and the app's settings (see Third-party services). Recordings are never sent over the internet by Recit.
Advertising IDUsed by the Start.io ads SDK to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings.

Files you choose. Recit does not ask for access to all your photos, videos or files. When you import a video or image, or pick a song, Android's photo picker or file picker lets you choose, and Recit can read only the items you select.

Your recordings and files

What a recording can contain

A screen recording captures everything shown on your screen while it runs, including notifications, messages and anything you type that appears on screen. Sound from other apps is captured only from apps that allow it (Android blocks capture from apps that don't, and from calls). Please check your recordings before sharing them.

Where they are stored

  • Recordings and exported videos are saved to the Movies/Recit folder in your phone's shared storage. Your gallery app and other apps you've allowed to see your media can see them there.
  • Editing projects, voice-overs, copies of images you add, the separate game and mic sound of recordings, and temporary export files are kept in Recit's private app storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • Your settings (such as quality and theme) are stored on your device.

Recit's private data is excluded from Android's cloud backup. Videos in Movies/Recit may be backed up by a gallery or cloud app you use, according to that app's settings.

Who can see them

We cannot see your recordings or projects: they never leave your device unless you share them. When you use Share, Android sends the video to the app you choose, and that app's privacy policy applies.

Third-party services

Recit includes the following services from other companies. They collect some information automatically when the app is used, under their own privacy policies. We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK (such as Google Analytics for Firebase or Crashlytics).

Start.io (advertising)

Recit is free because it shows ads: a full-screen ad before you edit or share a video, and a video or full-screen ad before you export. Ads are provided by Start.io Inc. ("Start.io") through its SDK. According to Start.io, the SDK may collect and share:

  • device identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (Recit does not give the SDK location permission, so it cannot read your precise location through Recit);
  • device information, such as model, operating system, language, mobile carrier and certain device settings, and information about installed apps;
  • your interactions with ads, such as views and clicks;
  • diagnostic and crash information about the SDK.

Start.io uses this information to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and personalise advertising, and, as described in its own policies, may use it with its partners for advertising and audience services. Learn more, and use Start.io's opt-out options, in its privacy and terms centre, Privacy FAQ and "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" page.

Firebase Remote Config (Google)

Recit uses Firebase Remote Config, a Google service, to receive app settings without an app update: for example, whether an update is available or required, and whether ads are turned on. To deliver the right settings, it automatically collects a Firebase installation ID (a random identifier for this installation of the app), your country, language and time zone, your Android and app version, and the app's identifiers. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play

Recit is distributed through Google Play, which processes information about your download and use of the store under Google's Privacy Policy. If you allow it in your device settings, Google may give us aggregated statistics about installs, crashes and app performance. These do not identify you.

How information is used

  • To provide the app's features on your device: recording, editing, saving and sharing your videos. This happens on your phone.
  • To keep Recit free: through the advertising described above.
  • To keep the app working and up to date: delivering settings and update notices through Firebase Remote Config.
  • To help you: answering your messages when you contact us.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on: providing the service you asked for; our legitimate interests in funding Recit through advertising, keeping it secure and working and answering your questions; and your consent where the law requires it, for example for the Android permissions above.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we have no recordings or account data to share. Information is shared only:

  • with the third-party services above, which collect it directly through their SDKs;
  • when you choose to share a video with another app;
  • if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, us or others;
  • with a successor if the app is transferred to another developer, in which case this policy or an equally protective one will continue to apply.

Some privacy laws, such as California's, may treat the data advertising SDKs collect as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out through Start.io's page linked above and by deleting your advertising ID.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Recordings and videos stay on your device until you delete them, in Recit's gallery or any file or gallery app.
  • Projects and other private app data are deleted when you delete the project, clear the app's storage, or uninstall Recit. Uninstalling does not delete videos already saved in Movies/Recit.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you and follow up, then deleted.
  • Data collected by third-party services is kept according to their own policies. You can contact them directly to ask for deletion.

Your choices and rights

  • Permissions: allow or revoke the camera, microphone, notification and overlay permissions at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your phone's settings (on most phones: Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads). Deleting it stops apps from using it for personalised ads.
  • Your videos: delete recordings, exports and projects from within the app at any time.
  • Stop all collection: uninstall Recit.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, the UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we don't hold your recordings or any account data, most of this is in your hands on your device. For anything we hold, such as a support email, contact us and we'll respond within the time the law requires. For data collected by Start.io or Google, please also use their tools and contacts linked above.

Children

Recit is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and is not designed for them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Keeping your recordings on your device, in storage protected by Android, is the main way Recit protects them. Recit's private files can't be read by other apps, and Firebase Remote Config uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please protect your phone with a screen lock.

International transfers

Start.io and Google may process the information they collect in countries other than yours, including the United States, with safeguards described in their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains new features or services. We'll change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or on Google Play. Please review it from time to time.
