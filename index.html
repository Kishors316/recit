Screen recorder

Everything you need to capture it right

Gameplay, tutorials, app demos or a moment worth keeping. Recit records it the way you want, with the controls out of your way.

One-tap recording

Tap Record, choose what to share, and recording starts after a short countdown. Stop from the floating button, the notification or the app.

Facecam overlay

Add your front camera as a round or rounded bubble. Drag it anywhere and pinch to resize, in small, medium or large.

App sound and mic

Capture game or app audio and your voice together. Recit keeps them as separate tracks, so you can balance them later.

Floating controls

Pause, resume and stop from a small button over any app. Show just a timer, or hide the controls completely.

Quality you choose

From 480p up to your screen's full resolution, at 30 or 60 fps, with four quality levels, in portrait or landscape.

Pause and resume

Skip the loading screens and the boring parts. Pause while you record and carry on in the same video.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Recit free?

Yes. Recit is free to download and use. A short ad is shown before you edit, export or share a video. Recording and watching your videos never show ads.

Where are my videos saved?

In the Movies/Recit folder on your phone, so they also show up in your gallery app. Edited videos are saved there as new files, and your original recordings are never changed.

Why is part of my recording black?

Some apps, such as banking apps and video streaming services, block screen capture to protect their content. Android shows them as black in every screen recording, whichever recorder you use.

Why is a game's sound missing from my recording?

Android lets apps record other apps' sound only when those apps allow it. Some games and apps don't, and for them only your microphone can be recorded.

Which phones does Recit work on?

Android phones and tablets running Android 10 or later. Recit adapts to tablets, foldables and landscape screens.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for or log in to. Open the app and start recording.
